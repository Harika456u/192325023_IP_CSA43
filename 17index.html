Welcome to VoteOnline

VoteOnline demonstrates an online election system for registered voters.

Administrators can manage candidates, elections and voter lists. Voters can verify their identity, select a candidate and submit a vote.

Lab demonstration: This prototype uses browser storage and simulated OTP. Do not use it for real elections or sensitive voter information.
